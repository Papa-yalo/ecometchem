Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This page explains what data EkoMetChem collects through this website (ekometchem.com), why, and how you can control it.

Who we are

EkoMetChem is an international trading company based in Poland. For any privacy-related question or request, contact us at info@ekometchem.com.

Contact form

When you submit the contact form, we receive your name, email address, company (if provided), and message. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties beyond the tools we use to receive it (our website hosting provider).

Cookies & analytics

With your consent (see the banner shown on your first visit), we use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic patterns — for example, which pages are visited and which materials are most viewed. This helps us improve the site. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to build individual advertising profiles, and no ads are shown on this site.

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site and reloading the page, which will show the cookie banner again.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you (for example, data submitted through the contact form) by writing to info@ekometchem.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
